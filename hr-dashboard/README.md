📊 HR Data Analytics Dashboard

An interactive and comprehensive Human Resources (HR) Data Analytics Dashboard designed to monitor workforce metrics, analyze employee demographics, track training investments, and evaluate department distributions.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights critical high-level metrics at a glance:
* **Total Employees:** 2,845
* **Total Training Cost:** $1.59M
* **Average Employee Age:** 34.2 Years

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights & Sections
The dashboard is structured into multiple analytical sections to provide deep insights into organizational data:

### 1. Workforce Distribution
* **Employees by Department:** Tracks headcount across *Production, IT/IS, Sales, Software Engineering, Admin Offices, and Executive Office*.
* **Employees by Job Role:** Detailed breakdown of specific positions (e.g., Production Technician, Area Sales Manager, Software Engineer, etc.).
* **Classification Type:** Distribution of *Full-Time, Contract, and Part-Time* employees.

### 2. Demographics & Status
* **Gender Distribution:** Visualizes the proportion of male and female employees globally and broken down by department.
* **Marital Status:** Categorizes employees into *Single, Married, Divorced, and Widowed*.
* **Employee Status:** Filters and tracks *Active* versus *Terminated* personnel.
